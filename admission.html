ENROLLMENT BLUEPRINT

Step-by-Step Admission Procedure

Follow our 4 straightforward stages to secure your admission at Apex Institute of Technology & Management.

1

Online Registration

Complete the digital application form below with your academic profile, 10th/12th marks, and program preference.

2

Merit & Entrance Test

Take the online AITMET entrance exam or submit your JEE Main / State CET / CAT scorecard for scholarship & merit evaluation.

3

Counseling & Verification

Meet our academic counselors in person or via video call to review branch fit and verify mandatory original certificates.

4

Seat Allotment & Onboarding

Pay the provisional enrollment fee to lock in your seat, pick your hostel room, and receive your Student Identity Kit!

DOCUMENT VERIFICATION

Essential Documents Checklist

Candidates must submit one set of self-attested photocopies along with original certificates for verification during counseling.

Class 10th Certificate & Marksheet

Serves as legal proof of date of birth and candidate name spelling.

Class 12th Certificate & Marksheet

Subject marks (PCM/Commerce/Arts) from CBSE/ICSE or recognized State Board.

Graduation Degree & Marksheets

Mandatory for Postgraduate programs (MCA, MBA, M.Tech applicants).

Entrance Exam Scorecard

JEE Main / AITMET / State CET / CAT / MAT / GATE scorecard.

Transfer Certificate (TC) & Conduct

Issued by the previous school or college principal.

Migration Certificate

Original certificate from the previous board or university.

Recent Passport Photographs

6 copies of color passport-size photos against a white background.

Aadhaar Card / Government Photo ID

Original and copy of candidate and parent/guardian Aadhaar.

Caste / Category / Income Proof

Only if seeking SC/ST/OBC/EWS quota or scholarship waivers.
